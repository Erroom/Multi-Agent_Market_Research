# Sample Research Brief: Food Delivery Market in Nepal

> **Sample output for demonstrating the app structure.** This is illustrative content, not verified current market research.

## Summary
The food delivery market in Nepal should be assessed through current evidence on customer demand, geographic coverage, restaurant partnerships, pricing, delivery reliability, and digital payment options. The sample data supplied with this project does not establish market size, growth, or market leadership.

## Competitors
No competitors are listed because the sample dataset does not contain verified competitor evidence. In live web mode, the agents may identify up to two competitors when supported by retrieved sources.

## Key observations
- Market size and growth should be supported by recent industry data before making quantitative claims.
- Geographic coverage, delivery fees, restaurant selection, and delivery reliability are useful dimensions for competitor comparison.
- Customer surveys and company disclosures would improve the quality of the assessment.
